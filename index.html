The problem

The world already agreed on the number. It just has no way to reach it.

Everything that can't be cut in time has to be paid to be removed somewhere else — and that payment is the carbon market. The money exists. The projects exist. What's missing is the connection between them.

Where it breaks today

  1. 01

    Fragmentation

    Sellers, buyers, registries, and verifiers operate in disconnected silos.

  2. 02

    Price opacity

    No standardized pricing mechanism; prices vary wildly by region and project type.

  3. 03

    Settlement risk

    Manual, bilateral credit transfer creates counterparty risk and settlement delays.

  4. 04

    No signal

    Corporate buyers struggle to trace credits from project to retirement.
